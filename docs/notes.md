## Notes on the numbers

- The plateau at ~43 C is the SoC doing its job; the frame rate stabilises there
  instead of collapsing.
- Battery temperature keeps creeping up even after the back cover settles, which
  is why a long session still ends with a warmer phone than the plateau suggests.
- Nothing in this table needed a cooling fan - that would be a different test.

The narrative version of this test, with screenshots, is on the blog linked from
the home page.
